Privacy at Node Folds

Your data. Your choices.

Vesper works without an account or a Vesper chat server. This policy explains what stays on your device, what is sent to a service you choose, and how we handle website visits, support messages and voluntary contributions.

At a glance: Vesper has no account system and Node Folds does not operate a server that receives your chats. Your app data and provider keys are stored locally. If you send a prompt to an AI provider, that request goes from your device to the provider you selected. The website and support channels have separate data flows described below.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Node Folds publishes Vesper and operates nodefolds.com. This policy covers the Vesper browser app, its installable version, the Windows app, public Node Folds pages, and the support channels linked from those pages.

Vesper can connect to external AI providers and to local model servers that you configure. Those services are operated independently. This policy describes what Node Folds does and the data paths we understand from the product. It does not replace the privacy policy of a provider you choose.

For privacy questions, contact privacy@nodefolds.com. For product support, use our support page.

2. Information stored on your device

Vesper stores app information on the device where you use it. Depending on your features and settings, this may include chat history, characters, saved memory, private chat state, preferences, provider connection details, API keys, and files or images that you attach. The Windows app also stores model files that you choose to download.

In the browser version, Vesper uses browser storage on that device. In the Windows version, Vesper uses app storage on that computer. Free features do not require a Node Folds account. The current released app does not yet offer account sign in. When sign in is introduced, free users may create an account if they choose; it will not be required for free features. Premium or Vesper Plus access will require sign in to verify access and enable any required premium key or service. Before account sign in launches, we will update this policy with the account information collected, how it is used, how premium access is provided, and whether any account feature stores or syncs data. Signing in will not imply chat sync unless that feature is separately released and described here.

Local storage means the information may be accessible to someone who can use your device or browser profile. Your browser, operating system, backup tools, extensions, or security software may handle device data under their own settings. Vesper does not promise that local data is encrypted. Use a device lock, keep your software updated, and do not store a key on a device you do not control.

If you clear Vesper's browser or app data, local chats and settings may be removed. Export anything you want to keep before clearing data or uninstalling. We cannot restore local information that was never sent to us.

3. AI providers and other connections

Connecting an AI provider is your choice. When you send a prompt, Vesper sends the information needed for the request from your device to the provider you selected. Depending on the conversation and feature, that request can include:

  • Your prompt and relevant messages from the current conversation.
  • Images, documents, or other attachments you chose to send.
  • Provider or model identifiers, request settings, and your provider API key as the credential used to make the request.

These requests do not pass through a Node Folds chat server. The selected provider receives and processes them under its own terms, privacy policy, retention practices, and usage limits. A provider may retain or use requests under the settings and terms attached to your account with that provider. Check those terms before sending sensitive information.

If you connect a model server running on a computer or network you control, Vesper sends the request to the address you configured. The operator of that server can access the information it receives. We do not operate or control that server.

Private chats are not added to Vesper's saved chat history or memory. They still need to be sent to the selected provider when you request a response, so the provider's handling rules continue to apply.

4. Models, downloads and external destinations

When you choose to download a Windows model, your device requests the model file from Hugging Face. The download request may reveal ordinary connection details to that service, such as your IP address, browser or app network information, and the file requested. Your chats and provider keys are not included in the model file request.

Models are made by their respective authors and may have their own licenses, usage rules, and privacy information. Review the model details before downloading. The same applies to any third party site or service you open from Vesper or nodefolds.com.

5. Website and technical information

Cloudflare delivers and protects the public Node Folds website. When your browser requests a page or file, Cloudflare may process connection and request information such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested URL, request time, and security or diagnostic signals. This processing helps deliver the site, prevent abuse, and diagnose delivery problems.

Node Folds does not currently use the public site for advertising or behavioral analytics. The site may link to services that have their own cookies, analytics, or privacy practices. If you follow a link to GitHub, Hugging Face, Ko-fi, Netlify, or another provider, that service's policy applies to its handling of your visit.

The support total displayed in Vesper is retrieved from a public Netlify function. The request does not include your chats or provider keys. Netlify may process ordinary request information to operate and protect its service.

6. Support messages and problem reports

If you email us, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards your message to our mailbox provider. We receive the email address and any content, attachments, or other details you include. We use them to read and respond to your question, investigate a reported issue, and maintain support operations.

If you report a problem through the Vesper GitHub issue tracker, the report is submitted to GitHub. Issues in the public Vesper repository may be visible to the public and indexed by search engines. Do not include API keys, passwords, private conversations, personal documents, or anything you do not want published.

Only include information needed to explain the problem. A useful report usually includes the Vesper version, device and operating system, steps that reproduce the issue, and what you expected to happen. You can redact names, account identifiers, and unrelated information from screenshots.

7. Voluntary contributions

If you choose to support Vesper through Ko-fi, Ko-fi processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Ko-fi sends our Netlify function a notification for a completed payment. To count each contribution once and maintain the public total, we store the transaction identifier, amount, currency, payment type, and timestamp in Netlify Blobs.

That support counter does not store the payer's name, email address, or message. Ko-fi may collect and retain information required to process a payment, provide a receipt, comply with law, or prevent fraud. Ask Ko-fi about information it holds in its own service.

We also retain the contribution identifier and amount in the counter to prevent duplicate counting and maintain the total. No automatic deletion schedule is currently configured for those counter records. The support counter is separate from Vesper chat data.

8. Cookies, advertising and analytics

Node Folds does not currently use the public website or Vesper to serve targeted ads, build advertising profiles, or run behavioral analytics. The public site does not ask you to create a Node Folds account.

Cloudflare or an external service may use cookies or similar technologies when they are needed to deliver or protect that service, or when you choose to visit that service. Vesper itself may use local browser storage to save app data. Browser storage is not a website advertising cookie, and you can clear it through the app or browser settings.

9. When information is shared

Node Folds does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information is sent to a third party when you choose a connected service, send us a message, open an external site, or make a contribution. The main service providers involved in the flows described here are:

  • Cloudflare for delivery of nodefolds.com and email routing.
  • Netlify for the public support total and payment notification processing.
  • Ko-fi for voluntary payment processing.
  • GitHub for public source hosting and issue reports.
  • Hugging Face for Windows model downloads.
  • The AI provider or local server you choose for model requests.

These providers operate their own services. They may process information in locations outside your country and apply their own retention periods and security practices. Review their current policies for details. We may also disclose information if a valid legal obligation requires it, or when needed to protect the safety and integrity of the services, consistent with applicable law.

10. How long information is kept

Vesper's local information stays on your device until you remove it, clear app or browser storage, or uninstall the Windows app. Node Folds does not have a copy to delete or restore.

Support email is kept in our mailbox while it is useful for responding, handling the matter, and operating support. Payment identifiers and amounts remain in the support counter to prevent duplicate counting and maintain totals. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule for these counter records.

Cloudflare, Netlify, Ko-fi, GitHub, Hugging Face, and AI providers may keep their own logs, messages, or records under their respective policies and legal duties. We do not control those retention periods.

11. Security and data limits

We use the services and controls available to us to operate the website and support channels. No online service, local device, or email system can be guaranteed completely secure. Vesper's local storage is subject to the security of your device, operating system, browser profile, and any backups you configure.

Protect your provider keys as you would a password. Remove a key from Vesper and revoke it with the provider if you think it has been exposed. Do not email a key or include it in a public issue report. For security reports about Vesper or nodefolds.com, email support@nodefolds.com and include only the details needed for us to investigate.

12. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose whether to connect an AI provider, which provider to use, what prompt or attachment to send, and whether to send us a support message. You can remove chats, memory, settings, and provider keys from the device where Vesper is installed. You can stop using a connected provider and manage or revoke its key with that provider.

Privacy rights depend on where you live. Depending on local law, you may have a right to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or an explanation of information handled by Node Folds. Send a request to privacy@nodefolds.com. We may ask for enough information to confirm what record you mean and protect other people's information.

We cannot retrieve or delete local app data because it is not sent to us. To access or delete information held by an AI provider, Ko-fi, GitHub, Hugging Face, Cloudflare, or Netlify, contact that provider directly. We will respond to requests about information that we control under the laws that apply to the request.

13. Children

Vesper is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Vesper account. Vesper has no account system. Some open conversation features are intended only for adults 18 and older.

If you believe a child has sent Node Folds personal information by email, contact privacy@nodefolds.com. We will review the message and handle it as required by applicable law.

14. Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when Vesper, our support channels, or the services we use change. The date near the top shows when the current version was updated. If a change materially affects a data flow, we will make the updated policy available on this page.

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@nodefolds.com. Product questions: visit the Node Folds support page or email support@nodefolds.com.

This policy describes current product behavior. Features in development, including account sign in, Premium or Vesper Plus, Image Studio, Video Studio, and device sync, are not covered as launched services. We will update this page before a new feature begins handling data in a different way.
