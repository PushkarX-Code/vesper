Privacy at Node Folds

Vesper is built to keep chats, memory and provider keys on your device. This page explains what stays local, what leaves your device when you choose a service, and how to reach us.

The short version: Vesper has no account system and no chat server. Node Folds does not receive your conversations or provider keys. When you choose a model or another connected service, the information needed for that service goes directly to its provider.

1. Who we are

Node Folds publishes Vesper and operates the Node Folds website. For questions about this policy or a privacy request, contact privacy@nodefolds.com.

This policy covers the Vesper web app, its installable browser version, the Windows app, and nodefolds.com.

2. Information kept on your device

Vesper stores your chats, characters, memory, settings, wallpapers and provider keys in app storage on your device. The Windows app also stores the model files you choose to download. Node Folds does not receive these items through a Vesper account or chat service.

Private chats are not added to saved chat history or memory. If you clear Vesper's browser or app data, the local information is removed from that device. Keep your own export if you want a backup.

3. Services you choose

  • AI providers. When you send a prompt, the prompt, relevant conversation context, attachments and your provider key are sent from your device to the AI provider you selected. That provider handles the request under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Model downloads. If you download a Windows model, your device requests that file from Hugging Face. Your chats and provider keys are not part of that download request.
  • Local model servers. If you connect a model running on a device or server you control, Vesper sends requests to the address you configured.
  • Payments. If you choose to support Vesper, Ko-fi processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Website, support and payments

Cloudflare delivers the Node Folds website. Hosting and delivery providers may process connection details such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested page, and request time to provide and protect the service. Node Folds does not use this information for advertising or behavioral analytics. Each provider's own policy describes its logs and retention.

The Vesper support panel requests the public support total from our Netlify function. The request does not include your chats or provider keys. For a completed Ko-fi payment, Ko-fi sends our function a payment notification. To prevent duplicate counts and maintain totals, we store the transaction identifier, amount, currency, payment type and timestamp in Netlify Blobs. We do not store the payer's name, email address or message in that counter.

If you email us, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards your message to our mailbox provider. We receive the email address and message you send so we can respond and handle your request. Do not include a provider key or sensitive chat content in a support email.

5. Retention and your choices

Local Vesper data remains on your device until you remove it. Support messages are kept in our mailbox while they are useful for responding and operating support. Payment identifiers and amounts remain in the support counter while needed to prevent duplicate counting and maintain totals. Hosting and delivery providers may keep their own logs under their policies.

You can choose another AI provider, avoid sending a prompt or attachment, remove local app data, or contact us about information in a support message or payment record. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. If local law gives you additional rights, contact us and we will handle your request under that law.

6. Children

Vesper is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Vesper account. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us so we can review it.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this page as Vesper or its services change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@nodefolds.com. Product support: support@nodefolds.com.
