Node Folds

Privacy Policy

Updated November 22, 2026

This policy covers Vesper (the web app, Android install and Windows app) and the Node Folds website. Vesper has no accounts and Node Folds does not collect or store your chats, prompts, API keys or personal profile.

What stays private

Vesper has no analytics, ads or behavioral tracking. We do not run a chat server. When you use the website or load Vesper, the hosting and delivery services that serve those pages may process basic connection details, such as your IP address, browser information, requested page and request time, for delivery, reliability and security. Node Folds does not use that information for advertising or analytics. Those providers handle their own service logs under their own policies.

What stays on your device

  • Your chats, characters, memory, settings and wallpapers.
  • Your API keys for the AI providers you choose.
  • On Windows, the models you download and run on your PC.

Clearing the app data or uninstalling Vesper deletes all of it.

Who Vesper talks to

  • The AI provider you pick. When you send a message, it goes straight from your device to that provider with your key. Their privacy terms apply to that message.
  • Hugging Face, only when you download a model on Windows.
  • A local model server you set up yourself, if you add one.
  • Our support counter, when you open the Support Vesper panel. It returns the public total and does not receive your chats or API keys.
  • Ko-fi, if you choose to support us. Ko-fi processes your payment under its own privacy policy. For completed payments, Ko-fi sends our counter a webhook. We store the transaction identifier, amount, currency, payment type and timestamp in Netlify Blobs to prevent duplicate counting and maintain the total. We do not store the payer's name, email address or message in that counter.

Model downloads

When you choose to download a Windows model, Vesper requests that file from Hugging Face. Your chats and API keys are not part of the model download request.

Photos and files

Photos and files you attach are sent from your device to the AI provider you picked, as part of that message. Review that provider's terms and privacy policy before sending sensitive material.

Children

Vesper is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

When you contact us

If you email support or privacy, we receive the address and message you send so we can respond and handle your request. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards these messages to our mailbox provider, which processes them under its own privacy terms. Please do not include API keys or sensitive chat content in a support email.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@nodefolds.com. For anything else, support@nodefolds.com.
