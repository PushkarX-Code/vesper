Privacy Policy

Updated November 22, 2026

This policy covers Vesper (the web app, the Android install and the Windows app, including Vesper AI on the Microsoft Store) and this website, all made by Node Folds. The short version: we do not collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Vesper has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not run a server that sees your chats.

What stays on your device

  • Your chats, characters, memory, settings and wallpapers.
  • Your API keys for the AI providers you choose.
  • On Windows, the models you download and run on your PC.

Clearing the app data or uninstalling Vesper deletes all of it.

Who Vesper talks to

  • The AI provider you pick. When you send a message, it goes straight from your device to that provider with your key. Their privacy terms apply to that message.
  • Hugging Face, only when you download a model on Windows.
  • A local model server you set up yourself, if you add one.
  • Our support counter, which returns the public support total. It receives no personal data.
  • Ko-fi, only if you choose to support us. Ko-fi handles that payment under its own policy.

Photos and files

Photos and files you attach are sent only to the AI provider you picked, as part of that message.

Children

Vesper is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@nodefolds.com. For anything else, support@nodefolds.com.
